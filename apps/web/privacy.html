Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

krypt is built so that we know as little as possible. This policy says exactly what reaches us, what we can see, and how long we keep it. “We” means krypt’s operator, in the United States.

  • We can’t read your messages, files or calls, or see the names you give people and groups.
  • krypt asks for no phone number, email address or account.
  • There are no ads, analytics, trackers or cookies. The app loads nothing from anywhere but krypt.chat.
  • We don’t keep logs of IP addresses.
  • We don’t sell or share personal information.

What stays on your devices

Your identity’s keys, your contacts, your conversations and their history, the names you give people, and your settings are kept in your browser’s storage on each of your devices. They leave a device only end-to-end encrypted. “Burn this identity” in Settings deletes them from that device, as does clearing krypt’s site data in your browser.

What reaches our servers

Messages and changes to conversations

End-to-end encrypted. We see each one’s size (rounded up to a multiple of 32 bytes), when it was sent, and the random address of its conversation.

Kept: 30 days after it’s sent, or less when a conversation holds more than 4,096 entries or 64 MiB. A conversation nobody has used for 90 days is deleted entirely.

Files

Photos, videos, voice messages and other files are encrypted on your device and stored in parts. We see their size and when they were uploaded, not their names or contents. Photos and videos have their location and camera details removed before they’re encrypted.

Kept: 30 days after upload.

Keys for starting conversations

Your devices publish public keys so others can add you to conversations. They include your identity’s public key, which is also in your contact card.

Kept: until used, or until they expire.

Your @handle, if you claim one

The handle, the contact card it points to (your identity’s public key and your inbox address) and your passkey’s public key. Anyone who knows the handle can look up the card.

Kept: until you give up the handle.

Your passkey backup, if you make one

Your identity and contacts, encrypted with a key only your passkey can produce. We can’t open it.

Kept: until a newer backup replaces it, or you give up the handle.

Notifications, if you turn them on

Your browser gives krypt an address at its push service (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft). We keep it with the conversations it’s for, and send it wakeups that carry no content: the push service learns only that something happened.

Kept: 30 days after your device last renewed it (it renews weekly), or until you turn notifications off or mute the conversation.

Linking a device, and invite links

Linking passes your identity to your new device through a one-time package sealed so only that device can open it. A request to join a group through its invite link is sealed so only the group’s admins can read it.

Kept: link packages until read or 10 minutes; join requests 30 days.

Typing and online status

Encrypted, passed to the devices that are online at that moment, and never stored.

Calls

Calls are encrypted end to end and connect your device directly to the other person’s when they can, so they don’t pass through us. A direct call shows each of you the other’s IP address. When a call is relayed (or you turn on “Always relay calls”), the encrypted call passes through Cloudflare’s relay instead.

Kept: nothing. Relay passes last 6 hours.

IP addresses

Our servers see the IP address of every request and use it at that moment to limit abuse: how many requests and connections one address may make. An address is held only while your device’s connection is open, and never written to our storage or logs.

What our servers can still learn

Even without contents, some information passes through our servers and Cloudflare’s while krypt runs: which conversation addresses a device reads and writes, from which IP address, when, and how much. Conversation addresses are random, not names, and we don’t record any of this. But someone who controlled the servers could watch it. From the timing of read receipts (which wake nobody), they could also tell roughly when someone reads.

Cookies and tracking

krypt uses no cookies and no tracking of any kind. Your browser’s storage keeps the app’s data and your settings, such as your theme and your privacy switches. That never leaves your device.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs our servers, serves krypt.chat and relays calls. It handles the information above and IP addresses for us, and may keep its own records of traffic under its own policies.
  • Your browser’s push service, if you turn on notifications, as described above.
  • The people you talk to see your messages and your contact card, and, in a direct call, your IP address. Anyone who knows your @handle can find your card. When you ask to join a group by its invite link, its admins see your card and the name you give.

When we share information

We don’t sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to profile anyone. We share what we have only:

  • with Cloudflare, to run krypt;
  • when the law requires it, in answer to valid legal process. We can only provide what’s described above. That never includes the contents of messages, files or calls, your contacts, or any name except an @handle;
  • to report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, as US law requires, or to protect someone’s life or safety in an emergency;
  • if krypt is sold or reorganized, in which case this policy still covers what we already hold.

Since there are no accounts, we have no way to tell you about a request for your information.

Your choices

  • Delete: “Burn this identity” deletes everything on that device; what’s on our servers expires as listed above. “Give up handle” deletes your handle and its backup right away.
  • Switch things off: notifications, read receipts, typing and online status, and direct calls (“Always relay calls”), all in Settings.
  • Ask us: write to hello@krypt.chat to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, wherever in the US you live. We usually can’t find anything about a person, because we don’t know who anyone is, but we can always help with a handle. We won’t treat you differently for asking. We don’t sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms.

Children

krypt isn’t for children under 13. We don’t ask anyone’s age and don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using krypt, contact us.

Security

Messages use MLS, the IETF standard for end-to-end encryption, with post-quantum key exchange (ML-KEM-768 with X25519). The app runs only releases we have signed. krypt has had an internal security review, not an independent audit, and no system is perfect. If a breach affects information we hold, we’ll give notice as the law requires; since we can’t contact you directly, we’ll post it at krypt.chat and in the app.

Where information is processed

We’re in the United States. Cloudflare’s network spans many countries, so information may be handled outside your own. If you’re in the European Economic Area or the UK: we process information to provide the service you ask for, for our legitimate interest in keeping krypt secure and limiting abuse, and to meet legal obligations. You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict its use, or receive it in a portable form, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We’ll post any change here with a new date, and tell you in the app before an important change takes effect.
